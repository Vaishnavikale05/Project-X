# Problem statement

Teachers currently lack a consistent, reviewable way to understand broad classroom participation/attention-related visual patterns across a recorded lecture. Manually reviewing long recordings is time-consuming and subjective. Project-X aims to produce post-lecture, classroom-level summaries from recorded video using explicit observable indicators, with source coverage and limitations visible to teachers.

The output is decision support for reflection, not a definitive assessment of an individual student or a measurement of internal mental states. Validation, privacy, lawful access, and human interpretation are prerequisites for deployment.
